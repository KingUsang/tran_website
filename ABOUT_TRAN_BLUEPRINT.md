**Status:** Draft for review

**Purpose:** Turn the brief's About TRAN requirements into an accurate, visually-led page plan before replacing the current `/about-us/` page.

The revision below supersedes the earlier card-led values/process direction in Sections 3–7 where they conflict.

## Revised narrative and visual direction

**Working direction: a founder-led story built across two markets.**

The page should read like an editorial company profile, not a sequence of cards. It opens with Quadri Omoloju's reason for founding TRAN, moves through documentary evidence of the early Nigeria school/community work, and shifts into the United Kingdom as CYTRAC becomes a physical product with global commercial ambition.

The UK and Nigeria should not appear as decorative flags or competing office cards. They shaped TRAN differently: Nigeria supplied direct community and school learning; the UK supports CYTRAC product development, public playtesting and commercial growth. CYTRAC is the bridge between them. UBI appears later as TRAN's separately labelled social-impact arm.

### Revised page flow

1. **Who TRAN is today** — educational IP company building children's digital-safety brands.
2. **Why TRAN exists** — Quadri saw that technical, fear-led digital-safety education was difficult for children, families and everyday technology users to retain.
3. **Learning in Nigeria** — schools, streets and communities became places to teach and learn directly from the intended audience.
4. **Birth of CYTRAC™** — community experience led to a physical board game children and families can play, discuss and remember together.
5. **Building across two markets** — Nigeria informs accessibility and direct education; the UK supports product development, London playtesting and commercial scale.
6. **The wider vision** — games, stories, animation, school resources, digital learning, competitions and other products, all clearly labelled as vision or development where applicable.
7. **How TRAN is structured** — TRAN as parent company, CYTRAC as flagship commercial IP and UBI as social-impact arm.
8. **Leadership and team** — approved current team only, with consistent portraits and concise biographies.
9. **Company facts and partnership close** — verified UK details, UK + Nigeria footprint and an organisational contact route.

### Revised visual rules

- Use founder, school, community, CYTRAC and London playtest photography as the page's main structure.
- Weave research, community learning, playtesting, educational design and product development into captions and story chapters; do not create six numbered process cards.
- Present mission, vision and values as one ambition statement and a short manifesto; do not use a grid of generic value cards.
- Use editorial transitions between Nigeria, CYTRAC and the UK; avoid maps, flags, counters and decorative timelines without verified dates.
- Reuse the homepage's connected TRAN/CYTRAC/UBI logic, but not its exact visual composition.

## 1. Page job

The page should make three things clear, in order:

1. TRAN is the parent educational intellectual-property company.
2. Its work has evolved from community education and digital-safety outreach into scalable, play-led products and experiences.
3. CYTRAC™ is the flagship commercial IP; Ubuntu Bridge Initiative (UBI) is TRAN's distinct social-impact arm.

This is not a second CYTRAC sales page. It should build confidence in the company behind CYTRAC for schools, partners, prospective collaborators, and media, then route each audience to a useful next step.

## 2. Narrative correction

The existing page is rich in history but currently presents TRAN as a broad catalogue of programmes. The rebuild should preserve the origin story while changing the hierarchy:

| Keep | Change | Do not repeat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Founder, team, community origins, real outreach evidence | Position TRAN first as an educational IP company, then explain its evolution | Legacy programme tiles and unsourced impact counters as the main proof of what TRAN is today |
| The practical digital-safety purpose | Show a visible company structure: TRAN → CYTRAC / UBI | Generic cybersecurity-consultancy language or fear-based imagery |
| Partnership/community credibility where it is still current and approved | Make CYTRAC the flagship commercial outcome and UBI the social-impact arm | Claims, dates, partnerships, registered details, or team biographies that cannot be verified |

## 3. Visual concept

**Working direction: roots, branches, worlds.**

The page begins with TRAN's root system: a sophisticated, dark parent-brand environment with a subtle organic network pattern rather than code, locks, or hacker imagery. As the story moves forward, one animated line branches into CYTRAC's brighter play-led territory and UBI's quieter navy/purple social-impact territory. It then returns to TRAN for the people and partnership close.

The visual metaphor should feel intentional but restrained. It explains the brand architecture instead of being decorative.

## 5. Intended page copy framework

This is directional copy, not final brand copy. It is constrained to claims in the brief and must be refined against approved company facts.

### Hero

**Eyebrow:** ABOUT TRAN  
**Headline:** Building the worlds where safer digital habits can begin.  
**Subcopy:** The Root Access Network is an educational intellectual-property company creating brands, products and experiences that help children play, learn and stay safer online.

**Primary CTA:** Explore CYTRAC™  
**Secondary CTA:** Partner with TRAN

### Origin

**Heading:** From shared learning to a bigger responsibility.  
**Body direction:** TRAN began through community learning and practical technology education. Listening to the people around us revealed a deeper need: children and families needed more approachable ways to build safer habits online. That insight led TRAN toward play-led digital-safety education and the creation of CYTRAC™.

Do not claim a founding date, a city, or a personal founder quote until these are approved.

### Mission and vision

**Mission:** Build the world's leading children's digital-safety brand.  
**Vision:** Make safety learning as natural and memorable as play.

### How we build

**Heading:** Ideas become experiences in the real world.  
**Supporting line:** We test, listen and design for children, families, educators and the partners who bring learning to life.

### Brand architecture

**Heading:** One parent company. Two clear routes to impact.  
**TRAN:** Educational IP company.  
**CYTRAC™:** Flagship commercial IP for play-led digital-safety learning.  
**UBI:** TRAN's social-impact arm, helping bridge emerging talent to opportunity, access and practical learning.

### Closing

**Heading:** Build the next chapter with TRAN.  
**Body direction:** For schools, commercial partners, licensors, funders and collaborators who want to make digital-safety learning more engaging and accessible.

## 6. Transitions and motion

Each effect has a communication job and must work without motion.

| Transition | Purpose | Reduced-motion equivalent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero → origin | A quiet root/path line begins beside the hero statement to introduce the company journey. | The line is statically visible. |
| Origin → mission | The founder/story image settles into a clean two-panel mission/vision composition. | Standard fade and background change. |
| Mission → process | The line extends into the six-stage process, reinforcing how TRAN works. | All process stages are visible immediately. |
| Process → structure | The path divides into CYTRAC and UBI, directly explaining the relationship. | Static diagram with explicit labels. |
| Values → timeline | Card accents become timeline checkpoints, creating a cohesive progression. | Simple stacked timeline. |
| Team → close | The visual system returns from people-focused warmth to TRAN's dark parent-brand foundation. | Immediate colour/background shift. |

Constraints:

- Use short opacity/transform reveals; no continuous parallax or scroll-jacking.
- Preserve keyboard order and visible focus states.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; content and navigation never depend on animation.
- On mobile, the brand-structure diagram and timeline stack into a clear reading order rather than requiring horizontal scrolling.

## 8. Information architecture and navigation

The new global header should use the homepage's newer architecture when the page is implemented:

- About TRAN (current)
- CYTRAC™
- Impact
- Partner With Us
- News & Stories
- Contact
- Secondary social-impact link: Ubuntu Bridge Initiative

Until the matching pages exist, any temporary links must point only to verified live destinations or clearly remain internal placeholders. The About page must not send users to an unrelated generic `Programs` page as its primary conversion path.

## 9. UX and accessibility quality bar

- Lead with a plain-language company explanation above the fold.
- Keep the origin story concise; use timeline/visual proof rather than long copy blocks.
- Make the CYTRAC/UBI relationship unmistakable in both text and visual hierarchy.
- Give every portrait a meaningful name/role, and make LinkedIn links optional rather than making a team member's entire card a surprise external link.
- Meet WCAG 2.2 AA targets for contrast, focus, form controls and responsive layout.
- Do not publish children/community imagery without confirmed permissions and appropriate safeguarding review.
