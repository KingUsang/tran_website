# About TRAN Wireframe

**Status:** Draft for structural review  
**Sources:** `TRAN Website Rebuild Software Brief.md`, `TRAN Founder's Story and Impact.md`, and the August 13 revision in `ABOUT_TRAN_BLUEPRINT.md`  
**Route:** `/about-us/`

## Design principle

The page is an editorial company story told through people, places and products. It should not become a stack of cards, numbered steps or disconnected corporate statements.

Desktop uses wide image/copy compositions and deliberate changes in scale. Mobile keeps the same narrative order and places captions directly after the image they explain.

## Interaction and transition notes

- Founder portrait and opening question can reveal together; no typewriter effect.
- Nigeria images enter as documentary spreads with static captions.
- CYTRAC product image may scale gently as it becomes the answer to the second question.
- The UK + Nigeria spread should crossfade only if both sides have authentic approved imagery; otherwise remain static.
- No numbered cards, orbiting counters, flag animation or decorative map paths.
- Every section remains complete with JavaScript disabled and under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Implementation blockers that do not affect layout

- Founder title and approved short biography.
- Current team roster and biographies.
- Greenwich/London playtest images and event context.
- Milestone dates and metric reporting/cohort references.
- Public UK company details.

These should be represented as internal placeholders during implementation and must not appear as `[CONFIRM]` text on the published page.
