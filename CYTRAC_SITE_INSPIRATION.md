# CYTRAC Website Inspiration Review

**Reviewed:** August 15, 2026  
**Live reference:** https://www.cytracgames.com/

## What the CYTRAC site communicates well

- It leads with a concrete family outcome rather than an abstract cybersecurity claim.
- It immediately shows the physical product and explains the basic buying facts: ages, players, duration and screen-free play.
- It uses a distinctive product palette: deep navy, bright blue, orange, mint and white.
- It moves from promise to benefits, trust, product detail, gameplay, email capture and questions in a clear commercial sequence.
- Its strongest sections pair real product photography with short explanations instead of relying on generic illustrations.
- The language consistently frames learning as family interaction, real scenarios and memorable habits.

## What TRAN should borrow

1. **Concrete language:** explain what visitors can see, use, support or buy.
2. **Authentic visual proof:** prioritise product, playtest, school, family and team photography.
3. **Clear audience routes:** parents, schools and commercial partners should each know their next action.
4. **Confident colour moments:** allow CYTRAC blue/orange and UBI purple to enter TRAN pages only where those child brands appear.
5. **Visible utility:** show formats, audiences, locations and partnership routes close to the relevant content.
6. **Better section rhythm:** alternate editorial storytelling, full-bleed photography, evidence and action rather than repeating cards.

## What TRAN should not copy

- TRAN should not become a second storefront; the corporate site must explain the company, portfolio, impact and partnership opportunity.
- Do not carry CYTRAC's rounded-card visual language into every TRAN section.
- Do not make orange CYTRAC calls to action the dominant corporate action colour.
- Do not copy product claims such as worldwide shipping, curriculum alignment, approvals, sales or playtest scale until evidence and wording are approved for TRAN.
- Do not present future CYTRAC ecosystem ideas as available products.

## Site-wide design direction

TRAN should feel like the parent studio behind credible educational worlds. Its base remains dark green, warm paper and documentary photography. Each child brand should enter with its own recognisable visual language:

- **CYTRAC:** playful, product-led, blue/orange/mint, family and school use.
- **UBI:** human, opportunity-led, navy/purple/white, access and community impact.
- **TRAN:** editorial, credible and connective; it explains why both exist and how partners can participate.

The result should feel related, not identical: one parent system with visibly different children.
