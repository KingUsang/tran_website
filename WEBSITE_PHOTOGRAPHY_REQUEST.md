# TRAN Website Media Request — Brief Minimum

The software brief does not require a large catalogue of new photography. It asks for authentic visual proof that makes TRAN feel like an educational entertainment/IP company rather than a generic cybersecurity organisation.

## Images needed

1. **CYTRAC being used**
   - One strong landscape photograph of children or a family playing CYTRAC for the homepage.
   - Two or three additional family or classroom-use photographs for the CYTRAC page.

2. **The physical CYTRAC product**
   - One clean hero photograph of the box and complete game.
   - Two or three close-ups of the board, cards and pieces.

3. **Real-world proof**
   - Two or three strong school, community or playtest photographs.
   - Include the London/Greenwich playtest if those originals are available.

4. **People**
   - One approved portrait for each published team member.
   - One or two participant, teacher or parent portraits only when they support a real story or testimonial.

5. **UBI impact**
   - Two or three photographs showing practical learning, mentorship, equipment support or a community project.

## Video needed

Video is optional unless a strong authentic clip already exists. Useful material would be:

- One short CYTRAC gameplay or product-demonstration video.
- One school, playtest or impact clip.
- One participant, parent or teacher reaction.

No founder video is required. Any published spoken video must include captions or a transcript.

## How to supply files

Send original or highest-resolution files. For each batch, include the approximate date, location, activity, photographer or owner, and confirmation that website publication is permitted. Images containing children require appropriate school and parent or guardian consent.

Quality and permission matter more than quantity. The same strong photograph may be cropped differently across multiple pages.
