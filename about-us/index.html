The Root Access Network

We build educational worlds for a safer, more skilled digital generation.

TRAN is an educational intellectual-property company creating products, platforms and opportunities across Nigeria and the United Kingdom.

Our story

Five milestones. One evolving mission.

What began as a community sharing cybersecurity knowledge has grown into connected worlds for play, practical skills and opportunity.

  1. 01Community roots · 2024

    Sharing cybersecurity knowledge.

    TRAN began as a community dedicated to making cybersecurity information easier to understand and use.

  2. 02Schools and communities

    Taking digital safety where it was needed.

    The work moved into schools and communities, creating practical conversations with young people.

  3. 03CYTRAC™

    Turning lessons into play.

    Community learning inspired a board-game concept that was prototyped, playtested and launched as CYTRAC™.

  4. 04Ubuntu Bridge Initiative

    Creating access and opportunity.

    UBI was launched to support emerging talent with practical learning, mentorship and digital inclusion.

  5. 05Skillverse

    Addressing the practical-skills gap.

    Skillverse was introduced to help secondary-school students leave school with useful digital abilities and project experience.

Mission

Build educational products and experiences that turn digital learning into confident action.

Vision

A generation equipped to participate safely, skilfully and confidently in the digital world.
