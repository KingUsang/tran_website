Our story

Six milestones. One evolving mission.

TRAN began with practical digital-safety learning and is growing that experience into educational products and social impact.

  1. 01TRAN formation

    A company built around accessible digital-safety learning.

    TRAN was formed to make digital-safety knowledge more practical, memorable and easier to access.

  2. 02School outreach

    Taking digital-safety learning into schools and communities.

    School-based activity in Nigeria reached more than 500 students and grounded TRAN's work in real learning environments.

  3. 03CYTRAC concept & prototype

    Turning everyday online choices into something children can play.

    Learning from community outreach led to the CYTRAC concept: a board game designed to make digital-safety conversations engaging and memorable.

  4. 04London playtest

    Testing the learning experience through public play.

    CYTRAC public playtesting in London helped TRAN observe how people interact with the game, gather feedback and refine it.

  5. 05Production & launch

    CYTRAC becomes a physical game, ready to play.

    The finished board game takes digital-safety learning beyond an idea and into shared play for children, families and educators.

  6. 06UBI launch

    Creating a dedicated route for access and opportunity.

    Ubuntu Bridge Initiative becomes TRAN's social-impact arm, connecting emerging talent with practical learning, mentorship and digital inclusion.
