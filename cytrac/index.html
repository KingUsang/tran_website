How it works

A round becomes a learning moment.

Every part of the game is designed to prompt a choice, a discussion and a safer habit—not more screen time.

01 · Set up

Start with a familiar board-game rhythm.

Players gather around the board and move through a shared digital-safety journey.

02 · Meet a situation

Recognise everyday online choices.

Cards introduce situations involving scams, privacy, passwords and online pressure.

03 · Discuss and decide

Turn play into habits that stick.

Children and adults weigh the choice together, making the lesson easier to remember beyond the game.

What children learn

Practical topics, without the fear.

CYTRAC makes room to explore the kinds of decisions children encounter as their digital lives grow.

Phishing & scamsSpot suspicious messages and pause before acting.
PasswordsUnderstand stronger account habits.
PrivacyThink before sharing personal information.
CyberbullyingKnow when to step away and seek support.
Social engineeringRecognise pressure, persuasion and deception.
Everyday choicesPractise judgement that can travel offline from the board.
