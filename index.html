T.R.A.N world 01

Play. Learn. Stay secure.

CYTRAC™ is a practical cybersecurity board game that helps children build safer online habits through play, discussion and real-life decision making.

At home, families can use CYTRAC to start useful conversations about passwords, scams, privacy, digital footprints and responsible online behaviour.

In schools, CYTRAC can be delivered as a one-hour, no-device classroom session, with an anonymous reflection and a short written summary of what students understood, misunderstood and should learn next.

For families, it is a game you can own. For schools, it is a practical way to start the conversation.

T.R.A.N world 02 • Digital skills learning

A practical pathway for the digital skills students need beyond the classroom.

T.R.A.N is building a structured digital skills platform for secondary schools, helping students develop capability across online safety, AI literacy, productivity, data, coding and digital creation.

The programme combines guided lessons, practical tasks, projects, progress tracking and certificates, giving schools a clearer picture of what students can actually do, not just what they have completed.

Teachers control lesson release, students move through learning at a manageable pace, and the platform is designed to work in real school settings, including shared-device and low-bandwidth environments.

T.R.A.N world 03

Access becomes opportunity.

Ubuntu Bridge Initiative runs a three-path internship programme in:

Cybersecurity • Digital Skills • Career Development

Participants gain practical experience through structured tasks, mentorship and real project work, with support designed to remove the barriers that can stop young people from taking part.

Support can include:

LaptopsPower banksData supportCertification vouchers

The aim is simple: help participants build capability, complete meaningful work and move towards further study, training or employment.
