About us

We make security
human.

The Root Access Network is a cybersecurity education company on a mission to make digital safety simple, practical, and accessible — for everyone, not just the few who can afford it.

Our story

From a mentorship circle to a movement

THE BEGINNING

A tech mentorship community

We started as a small group of people helping each other break into tech — sharing what we knew, freely.

THE TURN

Building CYTRAC

We built tools to teach internet safety — including CYTRAC, our board game that makes cybersecurity playable and memorable.

TODAY

The Ubuntu Bridge Initiative

Our social-impact arm — now a selective 10-week cybersecurity internship that drew ~3,000 applicants for 500 spots. Not a course; real, hands-on training that ends in a portfolio.

Our mission

To make cybersecurity knowledge as common as air

We foster cyber awareness and responsible digital citizenship — so people don't just hear about opportunities, they're prepared for them.

Our focus areas

Three pillars, one goal

Innovation

We design learning tools — like CYTRAC — that turn complex security ideas into things people can play and understand.

Mentorship

We connect novices with experienced professionals for training that prepares them for real opportunities.

Awareness

We take digital literacy into schools, streets, and communities — meeting people where they already are.
